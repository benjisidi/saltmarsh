# The Brians
Following the receipt of [[The Wizard Sanbalets Note]], we stop off at [[Curtly]]'s speakeasy to pick up some healing potions, and then head to [[Duncan's Head]]. The hill is topped by an old shrine.

Eventually we come upon a steep path, set into a small wooded ravine. On our way up, we pass some *magic mouth* runes shouting "Nyahh, you fools! This is your doom!"
